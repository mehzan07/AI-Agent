
NordicTech Solutions customer support is available Monday through Friday from 08:00 to 17:00 Central European Time.

Customers can contact support through the customer portal or by email.

Standard support requests are normally answered within one business day.

Critical technical problems should be marked as urgent in the customer portal.

An urgent request should include the customer's company name, contact information, product name, description of the problem, and any relevant error messages.

NordicAI customers can also use the AI assistant to find information in the product documentation.

The AI assistant should not invent information about company policies or product capabilities.

If the required information cannot be found in the knowledge base, the customer should be informed that the information is unavailable and should be directed to customer support when appropriate.

NordicAI is the company's main artificial intelligence product.

NordicAI can answer questions, search company knowledge bases, summarize documents, and assist employees with daily tasks.

NordicAI can use Retrieval-Augmented Generation, also known as RAG, to retrieve information from company documents before generating an answer.

NordicData is designed for business analytics.

NordicData can collect information from multiple business systems and present the information in dashboards and reports.

NordicAutomate is designed for workflow automation.

NordicAutomate can connect different applications and automatically perform repetitive business processes.

For example, NordicAutomate can receive information from a customer form and send that information to a CRM system.

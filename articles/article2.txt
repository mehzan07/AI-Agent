Our company is called NordicTech Solutions.

NordicTech Solutions is a software company based in Stockholm, Sweden. The company develops artificial intelligence solutions, automation systems, and data platforms for small and medium-sized businesses.

The company was founded in 2020.

Our main products are NordicAI, NordicData, and NordicAutomate.

NordicAI is an AI assistant platform that helps businesses automate customer support and internal knowledge management.

NordicData is a data analytics platform that allows companies to analyze business information and create dashboards.

NordicAutomate is an automation platform used to connect business applications and automate repetitive tasks.

The company focuses on secure, reliable, and easy-to-use AI solutions.
